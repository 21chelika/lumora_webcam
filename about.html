About Lumora

Capture Your Fan Moments — create memories that shine forever ✨

What is Lumora?

Lumora is a fan photobooth studio made for fans who love creating memories in beautiful and meaningful ways. Whether you're celebrating birthdays, anniversaries, comeback projects, concerts, or fandom events — Lumora helps you turn special moments into personalized photostrips filled with memories.

No complicated setup, no installation needed — just choose your favorite template, capture your moments, and let the memories stay forever.

The Meaning of Lumora

The name Lumora comes from the words Lumen and Aura.

Lumen symbolizes light — the little flash that captures beautiful moments.

Aura represents emotions, memories, and the warm feeling shared between fans and the moments they treasure most.

“Moments may pass, but memories can always shine.”

Our Mission

At Lumora, we believe every fan moment deserves to be remembered.

Our goal is to create a fun and beautiful space where fans can celebrate together, express creativity, and preserve memories through interactive photobooth experiences.
